Getting started

Three things to do this week.

The how-to guide covers every sheet in detail. If you do nothing else, do these.

01 · DAY ONE

Calibrate your probes first

Number every probe and check it in iced water and boiling water (Sheet 05). If a probe is 3 degrees out, every reading on every other sheet is wrong.

02 · DAY ONE

Put each sheet where the reading is taken

On the fridge, at the pass, by the receiving door. A log kept in the office gets filled in from memory at the end of the week.

03 · EVERY WEEK

Have someone senior sign it off

Look for gaps, numbers that are too perfect, and breaches with no action beside them. A log nobody reviews is a log nobody trusts.
