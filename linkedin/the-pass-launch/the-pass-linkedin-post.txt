Announcing The Pass, Kurokaa's kitchen standards practice for Kolkata.

Every owner in this city can tell you how last night went. Almost none can tell you whether the kitchen would have survived a food safety officer walking in at half past seven, mid-service, with a probe thermometer and a notice pad.

Not because anyone is careless. Because nobody has ever walked the room with a written standard in their hand and told them the truth. The chef is too close to it. The owner is looking at the P&L. And the first person to look properly is usually the one holding the notice.

Pujo is a few weeks out. Double the stock, temporary hands, the fridge that has been "a bit warm" since July. Everything marginal in September becomes critical in October, and that is exactly when enforcement is most active. We have been hearing the same fear from food businesses across Kolkata, and we can help.

The Pass is an unannounced review of your kitchen by a chef trained in the UK inspection system, where every kitchen's hygiene rating is public. Forty-four controls, walked from the delivery door to the plate. Every fridge probed, and your probe checked against ours. Thirty days of records read. Anything critical said out loud before we leave. A written report within seventy-two hours, and a thirty-day plan ordered by what is cheapest and fastest to fix.

We do this slowly, on purpose, and for a limited number of kitchens before Pujo, because it is done properly or not at all. And we do it with one intention: everyone wins. The owner keeps the doors open. The chef gets a standard that protects them. The customer eats safely and never knows. And the regulator gets what the whole system was built to produce: a kitchen that does not need closing.

In plain terms, so nobody misunderstands: The Pass is not an FSSAI-recognised audit, it certifies nothing, and your report will never recommend our software.

Fixed price, quoted in writing before we come. Half on booking, half on the report.
