The Pass is not an FSSAI-recognised food safety auditing agency. A review is not a regulatory audit, does not reduce your inspection frequency, and certifies nothing.

1. What a review is

A Kitchen Standards Review is an independent, unannounced operational assessment of your premises against our control set, carried out by a chef. It produces observations and recommendations. It does not produce a licence, a certificate, a rating, a score or any form of official approval.

Findings describe what was observed on the day of the visit. They are not a guarantee, warranty or prediction of the outcome of any inspection, licensing decision or enforcement action by any authority, at any time.

1.1 Scope

A standard review covers food safety and kitchen health and safety controls: receiving, chilled and frozen storage, cooking, hot holding, cooling, cross-contamination, allergens, personal hygiene, pest control, water and ice, cleaning, waste, records, and the physical condition of food areas.

Fire safety is expressly outside scope. We may note an obvious fire risk as a courtesy observation and will refer you to an agency licensed for that purpose. We do not assess fire safety compliance and nothing in a report should be read as doing so.

2. Booking and the visit window

Reviews are unannounced by design. On booking we agree a window — typically two weeks — within which the visit will take place. We do not disclose the date or time in advance. That is the point of the service.

You must ensure that during the agreed window there is normally a person on the premises with authority to permit the visit. If nobody with that authority is present when we arrive, clause 5.3 applies.

3. Fees, GST and payment

3.1 Fee

The fee is quoted and fixed in advance, in writing, before booking. Published pricing of ₹10,000 to ₹25,000 per location is inclusive of GST at 18% applicable to professional services. There is no hourly billing, and no charge for travel within Kolkata and surrounding districts.

3.2 Payment structure

  • 50% on booking, which reserves the window and is non-refundable except as set out in clause 5.
  • 50% on delivery of the written report.

A GST invoice is issued for each payment. Payment is by the link, bank transfer or UPI details shown on that invoice.

3.3 Serious findings are never withheld for payment

Any critical finding is communicated to you verbally on the day of the visit, before the balance falls due. We will not hold back a matter presenting a risk to any person because an invoice is outstanding. The balance is payable against the written report, not against your right to be told something urgent.

4. Delivery of the report

We deliver the written report within 72 hours of the visit, by email, as a PDF. It contains a readiness assessment in plain language, findings graded critical, major, minor or observation with a target date for each, photographs of findings, a thirty-day corrective plan, and a statement of anything we were unable to assess and why.

One follow-up call is included once you have read it. If we cannot meet the 72-hour window for any reason of ours, we will tell you before it expires.

5. Cancellation, rescheduling and refunds

5.1 If you cancel

The booking deposit is non-refundable. It reserves a working day and the travel associated with it, neither of which we can recover at short notice.

5.2 Rescheduling

One reschedule is included at no charge, provided you tell us before the agreed window opens. The deposit carries across to the new window. A second reschedule requires a fresh booking and a fresh deposit.

5.3 If we cannot complete the visit

If we attend within the agreed window and cannot carry out the review because nobody with authority to permit it is present, or because access is refused or the premises are closed without notice, that attendance is treated as your reschedule under clause 5.2. If a reschedule has already been used, the deposit is consumed and a fresh booking is required.

5.4 If we cancel

If we cancel, cannot attend within the agreed window, or are unable to complete the review for any reason of ours, the deposit is refunded in full within seven working days. If a review is part-completed at our election, we will either complete it at no further cost or refund the deposit, at your choice.

5.5 After the visit

Once the review has been carried out, the work is done and no refund of the deposit or the balance is available. If you believe the report is materially deficient, tell us and we will address it.

6. Confidentiality

Everything we see, hear and photograph at your premises is confidential.

  • The report is provided to you and to nobody else. We do not send it to any regulator, licensing authority, landlord, insurer, franchisor or delivery platform, and we are under no obligation to do so.
  • We will not identify you as a client, publish your name, or use your premises as an example or case study without your separate written permission, requested afterwards and free to refuse.
  • We retain the report and its photographs for two years, so that a later verification visit has a baseline to compare against. After that we delete them. You may ask us to delete them sooner and we will.

7. Photographs and your team

We photograph findings, not people. Where a person is unavoidably captured, their face is obscured before the image enters the report.

We speak briefly with members of your team during a review to understand whether a control works in practice. Nothing said is attributed to an individual, and no member of your staff is named in the report. These conversations are not an assessment of any person’s performance and must not be used by you as one.

Personal data is processed only to deliver the review, and is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

8. Your responsibilities

  • You confirm that you own or lawfully occupy the premises and are authorised to permit a review of them.
  • You will give us reasonable access to food areas, storage, records and equipment during the visit.
  • You will not ask us to alter, soften or remove a finding. We will correct a factual error, gladly and promptly; we will not change a judgement because it is unwelcome.
  • Acting on the report is your decision and your responsibility. We advise; we do not manage your kitchen.

10. Ending an engagement

We may end an engagement, including any ongoing or retained arrangement, if a critical finding is not acted upon. We are not inspectors and we will not report you to any authority. We will not, however, remain the name attached to a kitchen that has chosen to disregard a matter we identified as presenting a risk to a person.

Where we end an engagement under this clause, fees for work already carried out remain payable and no refund is due.
