Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · Operated by Oe Delli Belly · GSTIN: 19BAJPB6981R1ZS

Recharge Model

Kurokaa operates on a prepaid 30-day access model. All successful payments activate location access for the selected restaurant location for 30 days from the payment date. Pricing is ₹999 per location per 30-day period, inclusive of applicable taxes.

Each location is billed independently. A business with multiple locations is charged separately per active location.

Service Delivery Timeline: Upon successful completion of the payment via our payment gateway, location access for Kurokaa is activated instantly. A confirmation email and digital tax invoice will be sent to your registered email address within 5 to 15 minutes of transaction completion.

Refund Policy

Payments made to Kurokaa are non-refundable once access has been activated.

Once a payment is processed and location access is activated, we do not provide refunds — including partial or pro-rata refunds for unused days within the active 30-day period.

This policy applies to all plans and all locations.

Cancellation Policy

Kurokaa does not use auto-renewing subscriptions. There is no recurring charge, no auto-debit, and no ongoing subscription to cancel.

To stop using Kurokaa, simply choose not to recharge your location access after the active 30-day period ends. Your access will expire naturally and no further charges will occur.

Your operational data (menus, transactions, staff records) remains stored and accessible — it is not deleted upon expiry. If you recharge at a later date, you resume from where you left off.

Failed Payments

If a payment attempt fails but money is deducted from your account, the amount will be refunded according to Razorpay's refund timelines and your banking provider's standard processing period (typically 5–7 business days).

In the event of a failed payment, no location access will be activated until a successful payment is confirmed.

If you believe you were charged for a failed transaction and have not received a refund within 7 business days, please contact us at hello.kurokaa@gmail.com with your transaction reference number.

Free Trial

The 30-day free trial requires no credit card and involves no payment. No refund policy applies to the trial period as no charges are made.
