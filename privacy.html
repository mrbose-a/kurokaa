We use your information to:

3. Third-Party Services

We share data with these third parties to operate the Service:

3.1 Anthropic (AI Processing)

Your restaurant's operational context is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate floor management recommendations. This includes: order volumes, inventory levels, kitchen pulse states, and product names. No personally identifiable customer information is included.

Anthropic's privacy policy: anthropic.com/privacy

3.2 Supabase (Database)

Your data is stored on Supabase's PostgreSQL infrastructure hosted in Singapore (ap-southeast-1).

Supabase privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy

3.3 Razorpay (Payment Processing)

Payment transactions are processed by Razorpay. We store only your subscription status and payment dates — never your card or bank details.

Razorpay privacy policy: razorpay.com/privacy

3.4 Railway and Vercel (Infrastructure)

The application runs on Railway (backend) and Vercel (frontend). Your data transits through their infrastructure during normal operation.

We do not sell your data to any third party.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Storage Location

All data is stored on Supabase servers in Singapore.

4.2 Security Measures

  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Database access is protected by Row Level Security
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt
  • We never store payment card details

4.3 Data Retention

Your data is retained while your account is active. If you request account deletion, all associated data is permanently deleted within 90 days.

6. Kitchen Standards Reviews (The Pass)

The Pass is our kitchen standards practice. It carries out unannounced on-site reviews of restaurant premises, which involves collecting information the software service does not. This section applies only to that service, and is governed by the The Pass Service Terms.

6.1 What we collect during a review

  • Photographs of findings — equipment, storage, surfaces, drainage and records. We photograph findings, not people. Where a person is unavoidably captured, their face is obscured before the image enters the report.
  • Observations of practice, recorded against control identifiers rather than against individuals.
  • Brief conversations with members of your team about how a control works in practice. Nothing said is attributed to an individual and no member of staff is named in the report.
  • Copies or photographs of operational records you make available — temperature logs, cleaning schedules, pest reports, water test certificates, licences and training records.
  • Contact and billing details for the person booking the review, and the details needed to raise a GST invoice.

6.2 How we use it

Solely to produce your report, to answer your questions about it, and — where you book one — to give a later verification visit something to compare against. We do not use it for marketing, and we do not use it to train any model.

6.3 Who we share it with

Nobody. Your report and everything gathered to produce it goes to you and to no one else. We do not send it to any regulator, licensing authority, landlord, insurer, franchisor or delivery platform. We are not inspectors, we are under no obligation to report anything to any authority, and we do not.

We will not identify you as a client, publish your name, or use your premises as an example or case study without your separate written permission, requested afterwards and free to refuse.

6.4 How long we keep it

Two years from the date of the visit, so that a later verification visit has a baseline to compare against. After that, the report and its photographs are deleted. You may ask us to delete them sooner and we will.

6.5 Your rights over review information

The rights in section 5 apply equally here. Write to hello@kurokaa.com to request a copy of what we hold about a review, or its deletion.
